=========================================================
TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION: QUALITY OF SERVICE (QoS) & CONCURRENCY
=========================================================

1. INTRODUCTION
BlossomBot implements a proprietary Quality of Service (QoS) layer to manage high-concurrency bursts and prioritize sequential integrity for every user.

2. THE PARALLEL AI WORKER POOL
- 10-WORKER POOL: The bot spawns 10 parallel background workers to handle high-traffic bursts. This ensures that 10 different users can be processed simultaneously without backlog.
- VITAL TASK QUEUE: Overloaded AI requests are queued to manage Gemini's Requests Per Minute (RPM) limits safely.
- RATE-LIMIT THROTTLING: Workers introduce a 1.5s forced delay between tasks to stay within free-tier quotas while maintaining high responsiveness.

3. ATOMIC USER-LEVEL SEQUENCING (BUSY-WAIT LOCKING)
- PENDING COUNTER: A Redis-based `pending:{user_id}` counter tracks a user's active tasks. If > 0, all new messages are forced into the queue, ensuring they are processed in exact chronological order.
- THE BUSY-WAIT LOCK: Workers wait up to 30 seconds for a user's previous task to finish and save to Redis. This eliminates the "Selection Blindness" bug where selections were processed before search results were saved.
- FIFO GUARANTEE: This architecture guarantees that even in a multi-pod cluster, a user's conversation remains strictly sequential.

4. USER FEEDBACK & LATENCY UX
- Position Feedback: If the queue size exceeds 2, the bot informs the user ("Position 3 in queue").
- Thinking Heartbeat: For non-fast-path requests, the bot acknowledges the user immediately with "🌸 Analizez cererea ta...".
- Silent Queueing: For normal human typing speeds, the queueing is invisible, providing a premium real-time feel.
